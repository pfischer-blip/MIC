Speed of sound used: m/s. Sample rate: – Hz.

How to use. Turn the volume up and the ring/silent switch off. Press Start. Point the bottom edge of the phone (speaker grille and mic) at a flat surface such as a wall, and keep your hand off the grille.

Calibrate for best results: point the phone at open space (or straight up at a high ceiling) and press Calibrate. It records the phone's own speaker-to-mic sound and the room's fixed echoes, then subtracts them.

Movement mode ignores everything that stays still and tracks whatever moves (for example, your hand or a person walking toward the phone), and shows approach/recede speed.

How it works. The speaker plays a short chirp 20 times a second. The mic hears it twice: directly, and again after it bounces off something. Distance = speed of sound × time between the two ÷ 2. Measuring from the direct sound cancels the phone's unknown audio delay.

Limits. Phone speakers are not directional, so it reports the strongest reflector in a wide cone, not exactly where you point. Large flat hard surfaces work best; soft or small things are weak. On iPhone the web version can't choose which mic/speaker iOS uses.
